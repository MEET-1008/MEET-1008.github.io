Privacy Policy – DST Viewer – Embroidery

Last updated: 11 November 2026

DST Viewer – Embroidery ("the app") is a free app made by Meet Vaghasiya ("we"). It lets you open, play and share .dst embroidery design files. This page explains what information the app uses.

1. Your design files stay on your phone

  • The app opens .dst files that you choose on your phone or share to it from other apps (for example WhatsApp, Telegram or Files).
  • Designs you save are stored only inside the app's private storage on your phone.
  • We do not upload your design files to any server. We cannot see your designs.
  • When you tap Share, the file goes only to the app you pick (WhatsApp, email, etc.). That app's own privacy policy applies.
  • If you uninstall the app, all saved designs are deleted from your phone.

2. No account, no personal details

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, email or any login. It does not use your camera, contacts or location permission.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to keep the app free. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location (from your internet address)
  • Device type, operating system and app usage
  • Ad views and clicks

This data is collected and used by Google, not by us. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. Children

The app is not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

We do not sell personal data. The app itself stores nothing outside your phone.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
